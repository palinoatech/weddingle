Questions

What is a digital housewarming invitation?

A digital housewarming invitation is a web page you share as a link, usually on WhatsApp. Unlike a PDF or image, it can play music, show a venue map, count down to the date and let guests add the event to their calendar.

How do guests receive the invitation?

You share one link on WhatsApp, Instagram or by message. Guests tap it and it opens in their phone browser, with no app to install.

Can the invitation be customised?

Yes. Pick a design you like, then send us your names, date, venue, photos and the music you want. We build it for you, and you can also share your own idea.

How do I order?

Message us on WhatsApp with your occasion and the design you like. We will take it from there.
