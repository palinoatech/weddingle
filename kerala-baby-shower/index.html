The ceremony

What is Pulikudi?

A Kerala tradition held in the seventh month, when the family gathers to bless the mother-to-be and the baby.

The puli

The elders give Devika a warm drink of tamarind and herbs, prepared at home, for strength in the months ahead.

Blessings

Before the lit nilavilakku, the family blesses mother and baby, with new clothes and gifts for Devika.

The sadya

A traditional vegetarian feast on banana leaf, with payasam to end it sweet.
